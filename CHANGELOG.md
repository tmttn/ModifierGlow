## 1.0.0 - First release

- Lights up action buttons bound with Shift, Ctrl, Alt or two or more modifiers
- Lights up macros with [mod] / [nomod] conditions
- A colour, glow strength and animation for each modifier, with 12 glow styles
- Options to glow always, in or out of combat, or only with a target
- Optional dimming of the other buttons
- Works with Blizzard's bars, Bartender4 and Dominos
- /modglow opens the options
